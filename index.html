Who Is MS Dhoni?

Mahendra Singh Dhoni was born on 7 July 1981 in Ranchi, Jharkhand. He is one of India's most celebrated cricketers and former captains.

His journey was not easy. Coming from a middle-class family, Dhoni worked hard to pursue his cricketing dream. Before international fame, he worked as a Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) at Kharagpur railway station.

His determination, powerful batting, exceptional wicketkeeping and calm leadership helped him become a legendary figure in Indian cricket.

The Struggles Behind The Success

Small-Town Dreams

Dhoni came from Ranchi, away from India's traditional cricketing centres. He had to prove himself through domestic cricket and consistent performances.

Railway Job

Before international recognition, Dhoni worked as a TTE at Kharagpur. Balancing professional responsibilities and his cricketing ambitions was an important part of his early journey.

Waiting For His Chance

He spent years developing his skills in local and domestic cricket before making his international debut in December 2004.

Handling Criticism

From disappointing performances to difficult captaincy decisions, Dhoni faced criticism but continued to focus on his game and team.

The Journey Of A Legend

1981 - The Beginning

Born in Ranchi, Bihar (now Jharkhand), India.

2004 - International Debut

Made his ODI debut against Bangladesh.

2007 - T20 World Cup

Led India to victory in the inaugural ICC T20 World Cup.

2011 - World Cup Glory

Captained India to the ICC Cricket World Cup title and finished the final with an unforgettable six.

2013 - Champions Trophy

Led India to victory in the ICC Champions Trophy.

2020 - International Retirement

Announced his retirement from international cricket on 15 August 2020.
